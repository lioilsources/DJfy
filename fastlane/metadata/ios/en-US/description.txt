DJfy turns your phone into a DJ booth and a party jukebox.

DECKS
• Up to 6 decks with vinyl, seek, tempo and volume
• Crossfader between decks A and B
• EQ kills (LOW / MID / HIGH) and an FX pad: HI-LO, ECHO, REVERB, 8-BIT, ROLL synced to the BPM

SMART PLAYLIST
• Enter a band or a song and DJfy finds similar tracks via Last.fm
• Sorted by BPM for a smooth mix

PARTY
• Guests scan a QR code, search and vote in their browser – no app, no account
• Smart queue: votes, waiting time, BPM and mood lock
• Co-DJs, vetoes and blocking guests
• Auto-DJ with crossfade and beatmatching; NON-STOP mode adds tracks by itself
• Send the sound over AirPlay or Bluetooth

BPM data: GetSongBPM. Recommendations: Last.fm. Streaming: SoundCloud.

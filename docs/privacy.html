Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Overview

This policy covers DJfy (DJ Deckify), a DJ app by Oldřich Vořechovský (ol1n), on the App Store and Google Play as com.ol1n.djfy, and the macOS build. DJfy has no account and no ads, and we do not collect personal information. To learn which features are used and where the app fails, it sends anonymous usage statistics, described below, which you can turn off at any time.

Anonymous Usage Statistics

What is sent. Short records of moments in the app: it was opened or went to the background, which screen was shown (home, party or settings) and for how long, that a smart playlist search started and ended (whether the query looked like “artist” or “artist - song”, whether it found anything, how many tracks and how many of them are playable or have a known BPM), that a track was loaded onto a deck (from the playlist or the party queue, how long it took, which audio path played it, or that it failed), that a track played for 30 seconds, which effect was used (for example ECHO or ROLL), that a deck feature was used for the first time in a session (scratching, EQ, pitch, crossfader, an extra deck, TAP, slip), that a tap hit something unavailable, that an error was shown (its type), and for a party: that it started and ended, how long it ran and how many guests, tracks and votes it had, and whether Auto-DJ, mood lock or a co-DJ were used. Each record also carries the app version, platform (for example iOS or Android), app language, whether a party is running, and two coarse bands: roughly how many times the app has been used on this device (1, 2, 3–5, 6–10, 11+) and roughly how many days ago it was first used (0, 1, 2–6, 7–29, 30+). The full list is published in the app's source repository (docs/telemetry.md).

Nothing about your music. The statistics never contain a track, artist, title, BPM value, search text, playlist, party name or code, or the name of any guest.

Why. To see how many new users get from the first launch to their first played track, how long people stay, where search, loading or the party fail, and which features nobody uses, so we can fix them.

No identifiers. The statistics contain no name, no email, no account, no device or advertising identifier and no exact device model. Each session gets a random number that exists only while the app runs, so two sessions cannot be linked to each other or to you. The statistics are not used for advertising or tracking and are not sold or shared.

Who receives them, and where. The statistics are received and stored by the developer, on the developer's own server in the EU (Czech Republic). Nobody else receives them: no analytics company or other third party is involved, and they do not pass through the party relay. Sending them, like any internet connection, reveals your IP address to that server; the IP address is not stored, it is used only to derive your country. Your country is the only location information kept.

How long. Statistics are kept for 12 months and then deleted.

Offline. Without a connection, up to 500 recent records wait on your device and are sent later; older ones are discarded.

How to turn them off. Tap the gear icon at the top of the main screen and switch off Anonymní statistiky používání (anonymous usage statistics). This takes effect immediately: records waiting on your device are deleted and nothing more is sent, until you turn it back on.

Music Services

To build a playlist and play music, the app talks directly to these services. They receive what you search for and which track is played, together with your IP address, under their own privacy policies:

  • Last.fm (similar tracks and artists): Last.fm Privacy Policy
  • GetSongBPM (tempo of a track): getsongbpm.com
  • SoundCloud (search and streaming): SoundCloud Privacy Policy

Cover images are loaded from those services' servers. DJfy does not sign you in to any of them and sends them no identifier of yours.

Party Mode

When you start a party, your phone and your guests' browsers exchange messages through a small relay run by the developer on the same server in the EU (Czech Republic). It carries the party name, the queue (titles, artists, cover links, BPM), votes and the names guests type in. It keeps a party only in memory and forgets it when the party ends, at the latest after 12 hours without activity; it stores nothing on disk and keeps no log of guests. To limit abuse it counts requests per IP address in memory only.

Local Storage

The tempos the app has looked up are cached on your device so they are not fetched again. The statistics keep a few small values there too (whether they are switched on, the day measuring began, a session counter, the features used, records waiting to be sent), from which the bands above are worked out on your device. Deleting the app deletes all of it. We never receive it.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • Personal identifiers (name, email, phone number, account)
  • What you search for or play (only the music services above see it)
  • Precise location (only the country, as above)
  • Device identifiers, the advertising ID or the exact device model
  • Contacts, photos, files or anything outside the app

Third-Party Services

DJfy contains no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs. The anonymous statistics go only to the developer's own server, as described above.

Children

DJfy is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children: the anonymous statistics described above contain nothing that identifies a person, and they can be turned off in the settings.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised date before the change ships.

Zásady ochrany soukromí

Aktualizováno: 14. listopadu 2026

DJfy nemá účet ani reklamy a nesbírá osobní údaje. Abychom věděli, které funkce se používají a kde appka zlobí, posílá anonymní statistiky používání. Dají se kdykoli vypnout.

  • Co se posílá. Krátké záznamy o tom, co se v appce děje: spuštění a odchod do pozadí, zobrazená obrazovka a jak dlouho, začátek a výsledek hledání chytrého playlistu (jen jestli dotaz vypadal jako „interpret“, nebo „interpret - song“, a počty nalezených skladeb), načtení skladby na deck (odkud, jak dlouho trvalo, jestli se povedlo), že skladba hrála 30 sekund, který efekt byl použit, první použití funkce decku v relaci, klepnutí na nedostupnou funkci, typ zobrazené chyby a u party její začátek, konec, délka a počty hostů, skladeb a hlasů. K tomu verze appky, platforma, jazyk, jestli běží party a dvě hrubá pásma: kolikrát a před kolika dny se appka v zařízení používala. Úplný seznam je v repozitáři appky (docs/telemetry.md).
  • Nic o hudbě. Žádná skladba, interpret, název, BPM, hledaný text, playlist, název nebo kód party ani jméno hosta.
  • Bez identifikátorů. Žádné jméno, e-mail, účet, ID zařízení ani reklamní ID, žádný model zařízení. Relace má náhodné číslo, které žije jen za běhu appky, takže dvě relace nejde spojit.
  • Kdo je dostane. Statistiky přijímá a ukládá vývojář na vlastním serveru v EU (Česko). Nikdo další je nedostane a nejdou přes relay party. IP adresa se neukládá, slouží jen k určení země; země je jediný údaj o poloze.
  • Jak dlouho. 12 měsíců, pak se smažou.
  • Vypnutí. Ozubené kolo nahoře na hlavní obrazovce → Anonymní statistiky používání. Platí hned: neodeslané záznamy v zařízení se smažou a nic dalšího se nepošle.
  • Hudební služby. Last.fm, GetSongBPM a SoundCloud dostávají to, co hledáš a pouštíš, a tvou IP adresu, podle svých zásad.
  • Party. Zprávy mezi telefonem a hosty jdou přes malý relay vývojáře na stejném serveru. Drží party jen v paměti a zapomene ji po skončení, nejpozději po 12 hodinách nečinnosti; nic neukládá na disk.
